Your privacy

Privacy policy

Last updated: 1 November 2026

Elemental Keep is a small game-jam project. This page explains, in plain language, what information the game website uses and what choices you have.

What we save

  • When you make an account, we save your username, a protected version of your password, and when the account was created. We do not save your password in readable form.
  • If you send a score to the leaderboard, we save the run ID, score details, and date. Your username and scores are shown publicly on the leaderboard.
  • The application does not intentionally store your IP address or User-Agent in its own records. The web server and hosting provider may keep connection logs, which can include this information.
  • In multiplayer, your chosen player name and game messages are shared with the other player while the match is running. They are held temporarily and are not saved to your account.
  • Your browser saves a few display choices, such as the colour theme and leaderboard settings. These stay in your browser.

Why we use it

We use account details to let you sign in, scores to run the leaderboard, and short-lived multiplayer details to connect players. Basic request notes help us keep the site working. The website loads some styling from jsDelivr and checks GitHub for game updates; those services may receive ordinary connection information when your browser or the game contacts them.

How long we keep it

Account and score information stays in the website's database until you delete your account. Deleting your account removes your scores and sign-in records from the live database too. Backups or hosting-provider records may take longer to disappear. Multiplayer details are cleared when the match ends, a player leaves, or a waiting room expires.

Your choices

You can delete your account at any time. This also removes your leaderboard scores. You can also ask to see or correct information about you, or raise a privacy concerns.

For privacy questions or requests, use the project's GitHub page. Please do not put passwords or other private information in a public post. You can also contact your local data protection authority if you have a concern.

Changes

If the way the game uses information changes, we will update this page.
